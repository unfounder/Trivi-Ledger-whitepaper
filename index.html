Introduction

Traditional banking systems are outdated and often inaccessible to many. Trivi Ledger seeks to replace these centralized systems with a decentralized solution, offering financial services that are transparent, secure, and accessible to all. With Trivi Ledger, users gain full control of their assets while benefiting from advanced features like taxes, loans, and offline support—all seamlessly integrated into a single platform.

Wallet vs Trivi Ledger

Normal Wallet

  • Primarily used for storing and managing cryptocurrencies.
  • Basic functionalities such as sending, receiving, and swapping tokens.
  • Lacks integration with traditional fiat systems.
  • Limited support for merchant payments and QR code-based transactions.
  • No advanced financial features like loans, taxes, or offline transaction support.

Trivi Ledger

  • Built on a decentralized blockchain platform.
  • Offers payment solutions, automated tax deductions, loan issuance, and more.
  • Seamless integration with fiat-crypto bridging systems like UPI and Paytm for conversion between cryptocurrencies and local currencies.
  • Supports offline transactions with fraud detection mechanisms to ensure security even in low-connectivity environments.

How It Works

  1. Wallet Creation: Users generate a secure wallet with a unique cryptographic key pair, giving them full control over their funds.
  2. Transactions: Transactions can be initiated through QR codes or wallet addresses, and are processed securely and anonymously on the blockchain.
  3. Compliance: The system automatically calculates and deducts taxes at the point of transaction, ensuring compliance without manual intervention.
  4. Banking Services: Offers loans, fiat-to-crypto conversion, and rewards programs, making it a fully functional banking solution.
  5. Connectivity: Peer-to-peer synchronization allows Trivi Ledger to operate even in low connectivity areas, while also providing integration with local financial systems like UPI and Paytm for easy currency exchange.

Token Utility

Trivi Token (TVT) is the native cryptocurrency of the Trivi Ledger ecosystem. It serves multiple critical purposes:

  • Used for transaction fees within the system.
  • Unlocks premium features, such as priority processing and access to additional financial services.
  • Grants voting power to users, allowing them to participate in the governance and decision-making process of the platform.
  • Can be used as collateral for loans within the platform.
  • Rewards both users and validators who contribute to the health and growth of the network.

Tax Information

The tax system in Trivi Ledger is fully automated, ensuring transparency and compliance with government regulations. A 30% tax is automatically deducted from 60% of each transaction, creating a fair system where taxes are collected directly from the transactions. This decentralized taxation ensures all participants contribute to the financial ecosystem in a transparent and efficient manner.

Rules for Transactions

Transaction Rules

  • Tax Collection: A 30% tax is collected from 60% of all transactions conducted through the Trivi Ledger system. The tax is automatically deducted at the time of transaction and remitted to the government's fund.
  • Transaction Fees: A small fee (to be determined) is charged for each transaction to ensure network sustainability and incentivize validators.
  • Transaction Limits: There are predefined limits on the transaction amounts for both sending and receiving, designed to prevent abuse of the system and to ensure compliance with regulatory standards.
  • Loan Repayments: Loans can be issued based on Trivi Token collateral. Repayments must follow a defined schedule, and late payments incur penalties to maintain system integrity.
  • Fraud Detection: Trivi Ledger employs machine learning algorithms to detect and prevent fraud in real time. Suspicious activity triggers automatic alerts and may result in transaction freezes pending investigation.

Use Cases

  • Educational Payments: Students can pay tuition and fees using Trivi Ledger, simplifying the process and offering a secure, fee-less method of transferring funds.
  • Support for the Unbanked: Trivi Ledger helps deliver financial aid directly to underserved populations who lack access to traditional banking services.
  • Simplified Banking for the Elderly: With user-friendly interfaces, Trivi Ledger makes financial management easy for elderly individuals, who can make payments and manage their funds with minimal effort.
  • Merchant Payments: Merchants can easily accept payments via Trivi Token, benefiting from the low transaction fees and fast processing times compared to traditional banking systems.
  • Government Automation: Trivi Ledger streamlines processes like GST automation, creating a system where governments can easily track and collect taxes from digital transactions.

Security & Privacy

  • ECC-Based Cryptography: Trivi Ledger uses the most advanced elliptic curve cryptography (ECC) to protect user funds and transactions.
  • Decentralized Structure: With no central authority, Trivi Ledger gives users full control over their data and assets, ensuring their privacy and security.
  • Anonymous but Verified: Trivi Ledger allows users to remain pseudonymous, while still ensuring that transactions are secure and verified through the decentralized network.
  • Personal Cloud Vault: Users can store important documents and sensitive data in their personal decentralized cloud vaults, ensuring complete privacy and security.
